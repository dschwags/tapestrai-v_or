Think of it like this: AI analysis is like calling an expert consultant. Instead of tapestrAI paying for every user's consultations (which would require subscriptions or ads), you connect directly to the AI providers using your own account.

Benefits for you:

  • Privacy: Your images and analysis stay between you and the AI providers—never stored on our servers (we don't even have servers!)
  • Control: You see exactly what each analysis costs and can set your own spending limits
  • Transparency: No hidden fees, no subscriptions, no surprise charges
  • Free tier available: Google Gemini is completely free with no credit card required

Note: An API key is like a password that lets tapestrAI talk to the AI services on your behalf. It's stored encrypted in your browser and never leaves your device.
