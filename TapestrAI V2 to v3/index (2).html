💡 What is an API and Why Do We Use It?

An API (Application Programming Interface) is a way for different software applications to communicate with each other. Think of it as a messenger that takes your request, tells a system what you want, and returns the response back to you.

🤖 Why We Use AI APIs:

  • ✓ Expert AI Analysis: We connect to advanced AI models (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) that have deep knowledge of history, art, manufacturing, and cultural context
  • ✓ Your Privacy First: Your API key stays in your browser only. We never see it or store it on our servers
  • ✓ You Control the Costs: You use your own AI provider account, so you control usage and billing directly
  • ✓ Powerful Research: AI can analyze images, recognize patterns, and provide detailed historical context that would take hours of manual research

📚 How It Works:

  1. You upload a photo of your artifact
  2. tapestrAI sends the image to the AI provider (using your API key)
  3. The AI analyzes the artifact's visual characteristics, style, and context
  4. It generates a detailed report about history, significance, and references
  5. Results are displayed instantly in your browser

💳 Cost Note: Both Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude offer generous free tiers. Most users analyze dozens of artifacts before any charges apply. You can monitor usage in your provider's dashboard.
